About me

I completed my SSC in Commerce at Kamalpur MH High School with a GPA of 4.67, and 98 in Mathematics. Math was always the subject that made the most sense to me.

Because of my Commerce background, I could not apply for CST at university. I did not accept that as the end. I enrolled in the Diploma in CST at Dhaka Polytechnic Institute to start my technical career the right way.

My dream is to work in AI/ML and cybersecurity: using machine learning to detect threats, and making AI systems safe to trust.

I want to build something big in the future. For now I learn by building, and this site collects my projects, labs and notes from GitHub.
